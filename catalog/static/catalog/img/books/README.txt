BOOK IMAGES GO IN THIS FOLDER
-----------------------------
1. Copy your cover image here, e.g.  my-book.jpg   (jpg, png, webp, gif, svg)
2. In catalog/catalog_data.py, on that book's line, write:  "img": "my-book.jpg"
3. Refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R).

Tips: use simple lowercase names with dashes (no spaces); portrait covers
around 600 x 900 px look best.
